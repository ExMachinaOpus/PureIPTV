# Help and support

## Getting started

PureIPTV needs one thing from you: a source. That is either

- a **playlist link** (an M3U or M3U8 address), or
- a **server address with a username and password** (often called an Xtream Codes login).

Your provider gives you one of these. PureIPTV does not provide or recommend any sources.

On a TV you can type the source with the remote, or choose **Set up from your phone**: scan the QR
code with your phone's camera and fill in the form on your phone. The phone must be on the same
Wi-Fi network as the TV.

After that the app downloads the channel list, films, series and TV guide. A large source can take
a minute; you can leave the app while it finishes. Sources are refreshed automatically once a day.

## Settings

- **Interface language:** Settings › General.
- **Add, switch off, prefer or remove a source, or add a TV guide link to a playlist:** Settings › Sources.
- **Show or hide adult content:** Settings › Sources › Content.
- **About and licences:** Settings › Sources, at the bottom.

## Common questions

**A channel or film doesn't play.**
The stream comes straight from your provider. If it doesn't start, the provider isn't sending
video for it right now. Try again later, or try another channel. If the same channel comes from
two of your sources, PureIPTV switches to the other one by itself.

**Playback stops when someone else is watching.**
Many providers allow only one stream at a time per login. Stop playback on the other device, or
ask your provider for more connections.

**The TV guide is empty for some channels.**
The guide comes from your provider. Channels it has no guide data for show "No guide
information". If your playlist comes without a guide at all, ask your provider for its guide link
(XMLTV) and add it under Settings › Sources › TV guide link.

**Can I keep watching while using other apps?**
On phones and tablets, yes: press Home while something plays and it continues in a small window.
Close the window to stop.

**Is my connection encrypted?**
Only if your provider gives you an `https://` address. Most use `http://`, which is not encrypted.
See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy/) for what that means.

**How do I delete my data?**
Removing a source deletes its login and everything downloaded from it. Uninstalling the app, or
clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

## Reporting a problem

Report bugs and suggest improvements on
[GitHub](https://github.com/ExMachinaOpus/PureIPTV/issues). Say which device you use and what
you did.

**Never post your playlist link, server address, username or password**, not even in a
screenshot. They give anyone access to your subscription.

For anything you'd rather not post publicly, including security problems, email
[info@exmachinaopus.eu](mailto:info@exmachinaopus.eu).
