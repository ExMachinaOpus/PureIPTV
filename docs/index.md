# PureIPTV

An IPTV player for Google TV, Android TV and Android phones that just works.

Give it one thing, a playlist link or a server with username and password, and it figures out the
rest: live channels, the TV guide, movies and series, with duplicates merged into one library you
can browse with the remote.

**PureIPTV is a player only.** It includes no channels, playlists or subscriptions, and it does not
sell or recommend any. You add your own sources.

- No account, no adverts, no analytics.
- Your sources and logins stay on your device.
