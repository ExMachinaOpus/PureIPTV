## Content

PureIPTV is a media player. It contains no channels, films, series, playlists or subscriptions, and
it does not host, sell, link to or recommend any. It plays only the sources that users add
themselves, and connects directly to the servers in those sources.

Users are responsible for making sure they are entitled to use the sources they add.

PureIPTV and its developers have no relationship with any content provider, and no access to or
control over what any source contains. Questions or complaints about specific content should go to
whoever operates the source that provides it.
