PureIPTV is a player only. It does not include, sell or recommend any channels, playlists or subscriptions: you add a source you already have.

Give it one thing, a playlist link or a server address with username and password, and it figures out the rest: which entries are live channels, movies or series, which channels are duplicates, and how to show tens of thousands of entries as a library you can actually browse.

Made for Google TV and Android TV first, with full support for phones and tablets.

• One-step setup: type it on the TV, or scan a QR code and type it on your phone
• Several sources in one library: turn them on and off, and the same channel from two sources shows once, with automatic fallback when a stream fails
• Live TV grouped by category, and a program guide with search
• Movies and series with posters, plots, seasons and episodes; resume where you left off and go straight to the next episode
• Home with Continue watching, favorite channels and your list
• Search across channels, programs, movies and series
• Audio and subtitle choice, picture size per stream, surround audio on devices without their own decoder, and picture-in-picture on phones
• Everything works with the remote's arrows, OK and Back
• Built for very large playlists: 50,000+ channels and 100,000+ movies
• Clear messages when a stream doesn't work, never error codes
• Adult content hidden unless you turn it on
• 35 languages

Private: no account, no analytics, no ads. Your sources and logins stay on your device and are only sent to the providers you add.
